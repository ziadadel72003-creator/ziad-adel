## Verification methodology note

Same as Batches 1–2: direct `WebFetch` to `instagram.com` returns **HTTP 403**, so every URL was verified via **`WebSearch` cross-check** — the exact URL returned by an independent search with a crawled title/snippet matching the account and theme. Strongest signal = the account handle sitting in the URL path (`/{account}/p/…`); next = full caption visible in the snippet; standard = bare `/p/` or `/reel/` confirmed by a matching caption (ideally re-confirmed by a second query). Unconfirmed URLs were discarded, not guessed. Engagement counts are not exposed to this method.

Themes 11–13 are, by their nature (premium European switch brands; global lighting brands), mostly non-MENA global accounts — this matches the client's stated style sources. Themes 14–15 include a mix.
