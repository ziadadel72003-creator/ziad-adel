## Verification methodology note (read first)

Same as Batch 1: direct `WebFetch` to `instagram.com` returns **HTTP 403** for every URL, so each URL below was verified via **`WebSearch` cross-check** — the exact `instagram.com/…` URL was returned by an independent keyword search together with a search-engine-crawled title/snippet that matches (a) the account and (b) the theme keywords. URLs that could not be re-confirmed were discarded, not guessed. Engagement metrics (likes/comments) are not exposed to this method.

For this "Family & Security" bundle, MENA/Gulf accounts were prioritized and several were found (Oman, Kuwait, regional Mumzworld/Momcozy Arabic accounts, Bahrain), giving noticeably stronger Gulf coverage than Batch 1.
